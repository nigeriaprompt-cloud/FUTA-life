# Style & Social — features that make women stay (and make the world better for everyone)

**Status of this document:** these are well-grounded *hypotheses*, not proven facts about your users. Test them (§9) before building everything.

## 0. The idea in one paragraph
Men tend to arrive for the chance to meet people; women stay only if the world gives them (1) a reason of their own to be there, (2) control over who can reach them, and (3) friends. So the game must be good for women **on their own terms**: self-expression and status (fashion, hair, home), a safe social space they control, and a real-life payoff (real stylists, tailors, shops on the map). Do **not** treat women as bait to attract men: it feels that way to them, they leave, and the world turns into the harassment-heavy space that drives them off. Safety (§2) is therefore a launch requirement, not a later nice-to-have.

## 1. Style (the main reason to come back daily)
- **Deep wardrobe and hair:** Nigerian fashion first — Ankara, aso-ebi, gele, lace, native wear, streetwear, school/campus casual, bags, shoes, jewellery, glasses, caps, scarves. Hair: braids, plaits, cornrows, twists, locs, afro, wigs, bun, headwrap, natural and dyed colours. Makeup and nails (lip, eye, blush, lashes, nail sets) as avatar layers. Outfits can be saved (5 slots → more by level).
- **Fitting mirror and photo mode:** pose list (solo, duo, group), expressions, filters, a few frames/backdrops (campus plaza, golden-hour, club, beach). Exports a picture with a small watermark and the profile link (this is the viral loop, §6).
- **Style Feed:** each player has a "Lookbook"; they post outfits; others like and comment (comments follow chat moderation). Daily "Outfit of the day"; weekly **Best Dressed** with a visible winner board on a map billboard (e.g. at the junction) and an exclusive item.
- **Drops and seasons:** a new small collection every week; seasonal sets (Detty December, Valentine's, Eid, Christmas, Convocation, Freshers' week, Homecoming). Limited-time items create return visits.
- **Owambe and aso-ebi events:** a host picks an aso-ebi fabric/colour; guests who wear it get a bonus and a group photo spot. (Strong Nigerian cultural hook.)
- **Real-life link — "Try it, then book it":** real hair stylists, braiders, makeup artists, tailors, lash/nail techs and thrift/fashion sellers register as shops on the map (existing shop system + `building_links`). A player previews a hairstyle on her avatar, taps **Book / Order on WhatsApp**. Real businesses get customers; players get something useful. This is your difference from every other life game.

## 2. Safety and control (non-negotiable defaults — set once, enforced on the server)
1. **Messages from strangers are OFF by default.** Strangers can send a **Wave 👋** instead. A chat opens only when both people wave (mutual interest) or they are friends/in the same squad. No spam, no "hi" floods.
2. **Message requests inbox:** anything from a non-friend lands in a request folder with the sender's profile; accept / ignore / block. No read receipts for requests.
3. **Personal bubble:** other players cannot walk through you, stand inside a ~1.2 m radius (they are gently pushed out), or use contact emotes on you without your accept. One toggle turns the bubble up or off.
4. **Consent for interactions:** hug, dance-together, sit-together, kiss-emote-type actions are *requests* the other player must accept.
5. **Block = vanish.** Blocking hides both players from each other everywhere (map, chat, feed, search), including in interiors.
6. **Privacy switches:** hide online status, hide which building I'm in, hide friends list, profile visible to friends only. "Where I am" is never shown to non-friends, and never shows my real-world location.
7. **Report in two taps** from a name tag, with context (last 20 chat lines attached). Reports on harassment are handled first (target: a moderator reviews within 24 h; automatic temporary mute after N reports in an hour). Repeat offenders: timed suspension, then permanent ban by device and account.
8. **Chat filter:** profanity and sexual-harassment phrase list (Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and English variants) that blocks or blurs; players can raise the filter level. A "slow mode" per room.
9. **Moderators and ambassadors:** an in-world "Campus Prefect" role with a visible badge; recruit women as moderators and community ambassadors, not only men.
10. **Safe spaces:** an opt-in **Girls' Lounge** (a cozy interior: salon, chill area) and women-only events (self-declared, moderated, with a report button; it cannot be fully verified, so keep moderation active). Other lounges stay mixed. Do not promise "women-only" as a guarantee.
11. **No real names or real photos** required; usernames only. Optional "verified student" badge later (do not collect IDs without a clear policy and legal review under NDPR).
12. **Age:** ask for date of birth at sign-up; under-18 accounts get friends-only messaging, no Girls' Lounge/Club access, no gifting from non-friends.
13. **Gifting guardrails:** gifts cannot be tied to messages like "send me X"; a recipient can set "no gifts from strangers"; no cash-out of coins; no pressure mechanics (no "streaks" that charge you for missing a day).

## 3. Friends and belonging
- **Squads / crews (3–12 players):** shared name, badge, colours, a shared hangout (a rented apartment, §4), a squad chat, squad quests and a squad leaderboard.
- **Hangouts that make people stay:** salon, café, boutique, lounge, beach/park, rooftop, a "club" (music, dance emotes, lights; mixed but with the safety kit on). Seating clusters, group emotes, music player with Nigerian artists (use licensed or independent artists who agree; never ripped music).
- **Events calendar:** movie night, game night, book club, fashion show, karaoke, owambe, campus meet-ups. Anyone can host (RSVP list, host can mute/kick). Notifications 30 min before.
- **Newcomer path:** the first 10 minutes ends with a free outfit, a hairstyle, a friend suggestion (people with shared interests), and an invite to a small starter event. Players who find a friend in week one stay far longer.

## 4. Home, pets and decor (second strongest long-term hook)
- Rent first (cheap), buy later (the plots already in the plan). Decorate with furniture, wall art, plants, rugs, lights, a TV, a small kitchen.
- **Showroom:** visitors can see the outside; friends can enter. Guestbook and "like my home" counter. Home Tour of the week on the billboard.
- **Pets** (cat, dog, parrot) that follow the player and appear on the profile.
- Host parties: music, food items, a guest limit.

## 5. Earn, play, progress (small and friendly, not grindy)
- **Jobs and mini-games:** stylist, braider (match-the-pattern), baker/cook (jollof, puff-puff, suya), boutique owner (dress the mannequin), delivery rider, content creator (take photos for a "brand"). Pays a few coins, once per cooldown. Real shops can post real "gigs" later.
- **Daily/weekly quests:** wear an aso-ebi item, visit three friends, post a look, attend an event. Rewards are cosmetic.
- **Collections:** complete a themed set for a badge and an exclusive item.
- **Level and "style score"** from posts, likes, events attended, contributions. Shown as a small badge, not a ranking of people's bodies. **No beauty ratings, no "rate my body", no looks-based ranking** — rank *outfits* and *events*, never faces or people.

## 6. Virality loop (how it spreads)
1. Player builds a look → photo mode → **Share** (WhatsApp Status, Instagram, TikTok, link).
2. The image carries a code/link: "Join me in FUTA World".
3. A friend joins with the link → both get a limited outfit (**referral reward**), and the new player is dropped next to the inviter's squad hangout.
4. Weekly Best Dressed and Home Tour are shareable cards too.
Also: campus ambassadors with a personal code and a visible badge; school-vs-school (faculty-vs-faculty) fashion and event leaderboards; short "behind the look" clips are encouraged.
Track: invites sent → joins → day-7 retention, per source. Do not buy fake followers or fake players.

## 7. Monetisation that does not hurt women
Cosmetic coins (outfits, hair, makeup, home items, event tickets), limited drops, squad hangout rent, shop listings and featured shops for real businesses. **Avoid** pay-to-message, pay-to-be-seen, and any paid feature that lets someone bypass the safety defaults or the wave rule.

## 8. Cultural authenticity (the moat)
Pidgin and local-language lines, local food, danfo/keke, NEPA light-out events (the whole map goes dark and lamps/generators turn on), amala/jollof spots, owambe, Detty December, campus life (hostel life, SUG week, convocation). Ask Nigerian women creators to review items and events.

## 9. How to validate (do before heavy building)
- Interview or pilot with 10–20 women (students and non-students). Ask: what do you do in games like this, what makes you leave, what would you want to wear, who would you play with?
- Release the Style core + Safety kit first to a small group; watch: day-1/day-7 retention, share rate of photos, % of women among new accounts, report rate per 1,000 messages, time to first friend.
- If reports on harassment stay high, fix safety before adding features.

## 10. Build order (see ROADMAP, Phase 7B)
1. Safety kit (§2 items 1–8, 11–13) — together with chat in Phase 3 where possible.
2. Wardrobe, hair, makeup, photo mode, Lookbook/Style Feed, share card.
3. Wave and message requests; friends/squads.
4. Hangouts, events calendar, Girls' Lounge, aso-ebi events.
5. Home decor, pets, rent.
6. Mini-jobs, quests, collections, referral rewards, ambassadors.
7. Real stylist booking via the existing shop system.
